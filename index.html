PondToPlot · turning pond water into smarter field decisions

For small producers who farm a fish/shrimp pond and a crop field side by side (Bajo Lempa & Bay of Jiquilisco, El Salvador). Log your pond water, and PondToPlot combines it with NASA satellite data to tell you whether to irrigate with pond water, wait for rain, or exchange the water — and what to plant this season.
